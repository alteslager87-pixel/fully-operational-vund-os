📘 OFFICIAL INSTALLATION GUIDE (VUND-DOS GOLD EDITION)
This guide will help you move VUND-DOS from this repository to your physical hardware or virtual machine.

🛠 Method 1: Physical Hardware (Flash Drive) - RECOMMENDED
Use this method to experience the full power of VUND-DOS on real silicon.

Prepare your USB Drive: Plug in any USB flash drive (all data on it will be erased!).

Open Win32 Disk Imager:

Launch the application on your Windows machine.

In the "Image File" section, click the folder icon.

Crucial: Change the file type filter in the bottom right corner to "All files (.)".

Select vund dos.iso from your folder.

Write the Image:

Select your USB drive letter under "Device".

Click the "Write" button and confirm with "Yes".

Booting the System:

Restart your PC with the USB drive plugged in.

As soon as the screen turns on, mash your Boot Menu key (usually F11, F12, F10, or Esc).

Select your USB Flash Drive from the list.

Note: If it doesn't boot, enter your BIOS and enable Legacy Support or CSM Mode.

💻 Method 2: Virtual Machine (VirtualBox / QEMU)
Safe way to test the OS without leaving Windows.

Create a New VM:

Name: VUND-DOS

Type: Other / Version: Other/Unknown

Storage Settings:

In the "Storage" tab, find the Floppy Controller or IDE Controller.

Add a new disk and choose the vund dos.iso file.

Launch:

Hit Start and watch the VUND-DOS Monolith come to life in a window!

⌨️ Post-Installation: First Steps
Once you see the VUND-DOS > prompt, try these commands:

help — See all available 18+ commands.

system_info — Check the architect's credentials.

createimage — Enter the VGA Graphics mode.

Hotkeys: Try Ctrl + B or Ctrl + L at any time.
